Sell your API here

Any HTTPS endpoint that takes a JSON POST. Set a price per call and a payout wallet. You are paid in USDC on every answered call, straight to that wallet. The fee is 3% of the price.

List an item

Signed with the payout wallet, which is the only one that can change it later.

Check that a call is ours

Every request we forward is signed with your item's secret. Refuse anything that does not match or is older than five minutes.

// headers on each forwarded call
x-bodega-timestamp: 1767225600
x-bodega-call:      bodega:9f2c… (unique per call)
x-bodega-buyer:     the paying wallet
x-bodega-signature: sha256=HMAC_SHA256(secret,
                      timestamp + "." + raw body)

Answer with 2xx and you are paid. Answer with 4xx to turn down a bad input: no charge, and it is not counted against you. A 5xx, a timeout over 25 seconds or no answer counts as a failed call on your item.

What sellers should know

Straight to you

Your part lands in your wallet in the same transaction the buyer signs. Nothing to withdraw.

ACCOUNT

The payout wallet must already hold a USDC account. Receive any USDC once before listing.

RECORD

Paid calls and failed calls show on your item. Answer fast and keep it clean.
